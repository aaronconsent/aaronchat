Ideal customer profile

Who we build all this for.

Message-match beats clever every time. This is the one buyer we write and design for — and the 17 trades we tailor it to. When in doubt, write to this person.

The buyer

One primary persona. Everything on the site should sound like it was written for him.

“Mike” — the owner-operator

  • Owns a 1–15 truck home-service shop in East Texas
  • Great at the trade, not at marketing — and knows it
  • Wears every hat; checks his phone between jobs, mostly on mobile
  • Been burned by an agency that took a retainer and vanished
  • Judges vendors the way his customers judge him: show up, answer, do what you said

What he actually wants

  • The phone to ring with real jobs, not “leads”
  • A straight price and no 12-month trap
  • To reach a human when something breaks

What scares him off

  • ×Jargon, dashboards, and “strategy decks”
  • ×Fake urgency and too-good-to-be-true numbers
  • ×Being handed to an account manager
