# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Owner-operators of home-service shops, primarily around Lake Livingston, Texas, and secondarily any US service pro who finds the ads. One to fifteen trucks across 17 trades: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, general contracting/remodeling, fencing, concrete, lawn/landscaping, tree service, septic, pressure washing, pool service, garage door, gutters, pest control, painting, appliance repair. The buyer is the owner, on a phone, between jobs, with dirty hands and four minutes. He lives on the phone, distrusts agencies, and has been sold to badly before.

## Product Purpose

Hey Aaron! is Aaron Phillips's owner-operated marketing business for contractors. Websites, local SEO, Google Business Profile, ads, reviews, and speed-to-lead, all done by Aaron himself. The site's job is to get a contractor to **call** (primary) or leave a number for a callback (secondary), then convert to a client. The site is also the portfolio: it must demonstrate every tactic it sells.

## Positioning

The marketing guy who actually answers the phone. Twenty years running marketing for tech companies you've heard of, now pointed at your trucks, and you get Aaron, not a sales rep.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: a phone call to 713-384-8985, on every screen. Secondary: a 3-tap quiz / callback widget that captures a number for Aaron to call back.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: I book you jobs, not "leads," and I answer when you call.
- Belief ladder: (1) this is a real person, not an agency front; (2) he actually understands the trades and books jobs, not clicks; (3) he has serious credentials (cPanel, Monarx, Consent Resolve, 20 yrs); (4) the site itself proves he can build; (5) it's month to month with a real guarantee, so there's no trap; (6) calling is easy and low-risk.
- Proof on hand, all real: 20+ years; Chief Business Officer at cPanel; CMO at Monarx; co-founder & CMO of Consent Resolve; the live `/work/` portfolio of sites Aaron built. **No client testimonials, no invented metrics, no fake partner badges or certifications.** Pre-first-client for marketing services.

## Brand Personality

Blunt, confident, funny, self-aware, anti-agency. Ryan Reynolds meets Mythbusters. First person ("I", "me", "Aaron"), super informal, contractor-plain. Short sentences. Numbers over adjectives. Never corporate. Confidence comes from showing real work and answering the phone, never from adjectives about ourselves.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS/AI-startup (purple gradients, glass, 3D blobs, stock teams).
- Big-city agency gloss (impersonal, awards row, video hero).
- Cheap contractor template (clip-art, badge soup, stretched logos).
- **Fabricated proof of any kind** — the single hardest line. No made-up testimonials, client counts, certifications, or result numbers. The Google Stitch template we based the look on was full of these; they are all removed and must never return.

## Design Principles

**Show the receipts, never claim them.** Every number and claim is real and verifiable, or it's cut. Where something is modeled, it's labeled.

**The page is the portfolio.** Every tactic we sell runs live on the site, and we say so (the site is fast, the callback widget works, the reviews are real). Selling the tactic by running the tactic.

**Call-first.** The dominant action in every viewport is a phone call framed as access to a person. One dominant action per viewport, never competing CTAs.

**Honest urgency only.** True capacity and staffed-hours availability. No countdowns, no fake scarcity.

**Aaron is the brand.** Real photos of Aaron for trust (never stock, never AI for the founder). AI images are allowed only as clearly-labeled capability demos, never as founder/trust shots.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA, treated as the floor: 4.5:1 body / 3:1 large text, CTA-color text links darkened to 4.5:1, visible focus rings, skip link, `<main>` landmark, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation, accessibility statement with phone + email in the footer. Audience skews 40+ reading a phone outdoors.
