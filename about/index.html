The short version

I've been doing marketing for 20-some years, web hosting companies, email platforms, and for the last stretch, the trades. I ran business at cPanel, marketing at Monarx, and I co-founded my own software company. I know how to make a phone ring, at scale, with real budgets.

Then I moved out to Coldspring, by Lake Livingston, and got honest with myself: after 20 years of software, I wanted work I could actually see. A booked job, a truck rolling out, a hand to shake. My clients are roofers, golf cart dealers, and shop owners within a tank of gas from my house, plus a few who found me from farther out. I kept watching good contractors lose to worse ones online, not because their work was bad, but because their marketing was. So I built Hey Aaron! to fix exactly that, one shop at a time.

I've also been on your side of the table. I've hired vendors who overpromised, padded the invoice, and disappeared. That's exactly why the terms here look the way they do: you own everything, there's no contract, and I show you the work. And I'll tell you the truth even when it costs me the sale, if I dig into your shop and there's no easy win, I'll say so to your face.

“You’re one guy. What if you’re slammed?”

Fair question, and here is the honest answer. Being one person is the point, it is why you get me on the phone instead of a rotating account manager. But it also means I keep a real cap on how many shops I take on. When I am full, I am full, and I will tell you that straight rather than overload and do a bad job.

Your site, your Google, your ads keep running whether I am on a call or on vacation, because I build them to run. And you are month to month the whole time, so if I ever drop the ball, you are never stuck.
